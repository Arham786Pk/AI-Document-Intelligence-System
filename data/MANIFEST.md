# French Invoice Dataset – Inventory

**Scope:** AI French Invoice Data Extraction – Milestones 1 + 2
**Total documents:** 200 — **100 PDFs** (`data/pdf/`) + **100 images** (`data/images/`)
**Languages:** French only

> Restructured in M2 into exactly two flat folders. Every file was renamed to a
> consistent scheme; the old → new name mapping is recorded in
> [`pdf_manifest.csv`](pdf_manifest.csv) and [`images_manifest.csv`](images_manifest.csv).

## Layout on disk

| Folder | Count | Composition | Naming |
|--------|-------|-------------|--------|
| `data/pdf/` | 100 | 85 real + 15 synthetic | `FR_invoice_real_001…085.pdf`, `FR_invoice_synth_001…015.pdf` |
| `data/images/` | 100 | 31 real + 69 synthetic | `FR_invoice_img_real_001…031`, `FR_invoice_img_synth_001…069.png` |

No sub-folders inside either — real vs synthetic is distinguished by filename only.

### Real PDFs (85)
- 28 scanned receipts/invoices (orig. `inv*.pdf`)
- 5 scanned M1 invoices (orig. `Invoice_FR_016…020_scanned_*.pdf`)
- 52 sourced-internet French invoices: Factur-X / EN16931 reference invoices,
  Chorus Pro government test invoices, EDF/SFR/Euresto bills, etc.

### Real images (31)
- Phone photos of French receipts and invoices (orig. `IMG-20260507-WA0143…0174`).

### Synthetic (84 total: 15 PDF + 69 image)
- Generated by [`scripts/generate_synthetic_invoices.py`](../scripts/generate_synthetic_invoices.py)
  with reportlab + Faker (fr_FR). Each contains **all 12 entities** including
  `consumer_name`.

## Quarantine (excluded from dataset)

`quarantine/` holds 44 files removed from the training set, preserved for
traceability: 18 non-invoices (guides/FAQs), 23 low-quality (≤6/12 entities),
and 3 real-PII PDFs (`quarantine/pii_review/`, git-ignored — never pushed).
